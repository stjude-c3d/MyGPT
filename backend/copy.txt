To answer this question, we need to identify the samples that have a certain combination of conditions.

Let's assume that we want to find all samples where:

1. The "Mutation" is either "V600E" or "V600K"
2. The "Cancer" is either "THYR" (Thyroid) or "SKN" (Skin)
3. The "Tumor mutant allele fraction" is greater than 0.5

Here's the SQL query to achieve this:
```sql
SELECT *
FROM table_name
WHERE Mutation IN ('V600E', 'V600K') 
AND Cancer IN ('THYR', 'SKN') 
AND Tumor_mutation_allele_fraction > 0.5;
```
